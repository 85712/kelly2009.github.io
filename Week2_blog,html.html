What I Learned This Week

This week, I learned new things about using Pantheon and creating content for a website. I enjoyed learning how to make posts and create events because these activities helped me understand how websites can be updated and organized.

I also learned that it is important to complete and submit my work on time so I can get the grades I am working towards.

My Favourite Activity: Making Posts on Pantheon

One of my favourite things I learned this week was how to create a post on Pantheon. I enjoyed learning how to add content to a website and organize information so that other people can read it.

I liked this activity because it gave me a chance to practise working with a real website. It also helped me become more comfortable using Pantheon and understanding how posts are created.

Learning How to Create Events

Another activity I enjoyed was learning how to create events. I found it interesting to learn how events can be added to a website to share information about activities and important dates.

This was useful because I learned another way to add information to a website. I enjoyed trying something new and improving my skills while working with Pantheon.

Something I Did Not Like

One thing I did not like this week was not getting my full grade because I did not turn in my work. I was disappointed because I realized that even if I understand the activity, I still need to submit my assignments to receive the grade I want.

This taught me that responsibility and time management are important. Next time, I will try to keep track of my assignments, finish them on time, and make sure I submit my work before the deadline.

A Challenge I Faced

One thing I found difficult was finding information for next week's presentation. I had trouble finding the information I needed, which made preparing for the presentation more challenging.

This helped me realize that researching a topic takes time and patience. I need to look at different sources, choose information that is useful, and organize my ideas before making my presentation. Next time, I will start researching earlier and ask for help if I cannot find what I need.

What I Learned

This week, I learned how to create posts and events on Pantheon. I also learned that submitting work on time is important and that research can sometimes be difficult. I want to improve my organization, time management, and research skills.

My Overall Reflection

Overall, I enjoyed learning new things this week, especially how to make posts on Pantheon and create events. These activities helped me gain more experience with website management.

Even though I was disappointed that I did not get my full grade because I did not submit my work, I learned from the experience. I also know that finding information for my next presentation is something I need to work on.

Next week, I want to manage my time better, submit all my assignments, and do more research so I can feel prepared for my presentation. I look forward to learning more about websites and improving my skills.
