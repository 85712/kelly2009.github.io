How I Enjoyed Last Week

Last week was a fun and interesting week in class. I enjoyed learning new things about HTML and taking part in different activities. Some of my favourite parts were the semantic tags assignment and the LEGO activity we did together.

My Favourite Assignment: Semantic Tags

One of my favourite parts of last week was the assignment where we had to use semantic tags in HTML. I enjoyed learning how tags such as header, main, article and footer help organize a webpage.

This assignment helped me understand how to structure a website properly and improve my HTML skills. I found it interesting to see how different tags have different purposes.

The LEGO Activity

I also really enjoyed the LEGO activity we did in class. It was fun and different from our usual work. I liked the fact that i was able to create my own robot and taking part in a hands-on activity because it made learning more creative and enjoyable.

A Challenge I Faced

One thing I found difficult last week was finding the correct information for my presentation about one of the HTML tags. I struggled to find the information I needed, which made preparing my presentation more challenging.

This taught me that research can sometimes be difficult and that I need to look carefully at different sources to find reliable information. Next time, I will try to organise my research better and ask for help if I get stuck.

What I Learned

I learned more about semantic HTML tags, enjoyed working on creative activities, and realized that finding reliable information is an important skill.

My Overall Reflection

Overall, I enjoyed last week because I learned new things, completed interesting assignments and took part in fun activities. Although I found researching my HTML tag difficult, I know I can improve by practising and asking for help when needed. I look forward to learning more about HTML and web development in the future.
